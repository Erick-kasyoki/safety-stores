Repeating invoices

New invoices are created and marked as sent automatically on each date. They are created when you open this tab, or by app/cron_invoices.php if you schedule it (see the README).

You add workers here by name and email. Then the worker opens worker.html (open it), chooses Sign up and creates their own password. Only emails you add here can sign up. After that the worker signs in at worker.html and sees the orders customers place, can take an order and move it along (confirmed, dispatched, delivered), and can update stock. Workers cannot change prices, delete products, see costs or invoices, or manage other workers. Switch an account off to block it at once.

Customers sign up in the store. Here you can edit their details, set a new password, block them or delete them. Deleting a customer also deletes their orders.

Admins can do everything. You cannot switch off or delete your own account, and at least one admin must always stay active.

Store details

These show on the store and on invoices. Saving here overrides config.php.
